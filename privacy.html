Privacy Policy

At Liberty Trust Capital, we are committed to protecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal information. As a modern online bank affiliated with Space x, we prioritize transparency, security, and trust in how we handle your data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our website, mobile app, or any of our banking services (collectively, the “Services”).

By accessing or using our Services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, improve, and secure our Services. This includes:

Personal Information You Provide:

  • Identification details (e.g., name, date of birth, Social Security Number or equivalent, address, phone number, email).
  • Financial information (e.g., bank account details, transaction history, income).
  • Account credentials and preferences.

Information Collected Automatically:

  • Device and usage data (e.g., IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers).
  • Log data (e.g., access times, pages viewed, features used).
  • Cookies and similar technologies (e.g., to remember your preferences and enhance your experience).

Information from Third Parties:

  • Verification data from credit bureaus, identity verification services, or our banking partners.
  • Information from Space x as part of our affiliation for enhanced security and compliance.

We do not collect sensitive personal information (e.g., racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, health data) unless required for specific services and with your explicit consent.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and manage your account and Services (e.g., process transactions, deposits, transfers).
  • Verify your identity and prevent fraud.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements (e.g., anti-money laundering laws).
  • Improve our Services, develop new features, and conduct analytics.
  • Communicate with you (e.g., account updates, security alerts, customer support).
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it in limited circumstances:

  • With Our Affiliates and Partners: Including Space x for operational support, security, and regulatory purposes.
  • Service Providers: With trusted third parties who are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • For Legal Reasons: To comply with laws, respond to legal processes, protect our rights, or prevent fraud/harm.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.

4. Security of Your Information

  • Bank-level encryption for data in transit and at rest.
  • Biometric authentication and multi-factor authentication options.
  • Real-time fraud monitoring and alerts.
  • Strict access controls and regular security audits.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access, update, or delete your data.
  • Opt out of marketing emails.
  • Manage cookies through browser settings.
  • Request details on how we process your data.
