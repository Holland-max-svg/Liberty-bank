About Us

Welcome to Liberty Trust Capital– Your Trusted Modern Online Bank

At Liberty Trust Capital, we’re redefining banking for the digital age. As a fully online bank, we deliver seamless, secure, and innovative financial services through our intuitive mobile app and website – no branches, no hidden fees, just straightforward banking tailored to your lifestyle.

Liberty Trust Capital is proudly affiliated with Space X, a renowned and established institution with a long-standing reputation for excellence, stability, and trust in the financial world. This powerful partnership forms the backbone of our operations, providing unparalleled security, regulatory compliance, and financial backing. By leveraging Space X’s decades of expertise and proven track record, we combine the agility of modern fintech with the reliability of traditional banking – giving you the best of both worlds.

Our Mission

To make banking simple, transparent, and empowering for everyone. We eliminate unnecessary costs and complexity, offering fee-free accounts, real-time insights, and advanced tools to help you take control of your finances.

Our Story

Founded in Year, 2015 by a team of fintech visionaries, Liberty Trust Capital was created to address the frustrations of outdated banking systems. With the strong support and affiliation of Space X, we’ve rapidly grown into a trusted platform serving thousands of customers who value innovation backed by solid foundations.
